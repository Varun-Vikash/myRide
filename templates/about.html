myRide@BIT
myRide@BIT is an online interactive transit and navigation platform covering all places, blocks, and stops in Bannari Amman Institute of Technology (BIT Sathy). Featuring high-precision 2D campus navigation, real-time 60 FPS electric shuttle bus tracking, and Dijkstra-based shortest routing.
